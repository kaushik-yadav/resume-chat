Data Scientist, Payments Risk - Finlytics (fictional company)

About the role
Finlytics is hiring a Data Scientist to join the Payments Risk team. You will build models that
detect fraudulent transactions and help product teams make data-driven decisions.

Responsibilities
- Build, validate and deploy machine learning models for fraud detection and risk scoring
- Design and analyze A/B tests and communicate results to product and engineering partners
- Write production-quality Python and SQL; own data pipelines that feed models
- Partner with engineers to serve models through low-latency APIs
- Monitor model performance and data drift in production

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in data science, analytics or ML engineering
- Strong Python (pandas, scikit-learn) and SQL skills
- Experience with experimentation and statistical inference
- Experience with cloud data warehouses such as BigQuery or Snowflake
- Clear communication with non-technical stakeholders

Nice to have
- Experience in payments, fintech or fraud
- Familiarity with Airflow or other orchestration tools
- Experience with PyTorch or gradient boosting libraries (XGBoost, LightGBM)
- Docker and AWS
