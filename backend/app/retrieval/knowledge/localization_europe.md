# Hiring conventions: Europe

## United Kingdom (en-GB)
<!-- tags: en-GB, uk, united kingdom, britain, england, cv -->
Document name: "CV". Typically two pages. No photo, no date of birth, no marital status (Equality Act norms). Opens with a short "Personal profile" or "Profile" statement. British spelling: optimise, organisation, analyse, programme (for non-software programmes), centre. Dates "Jan 2022 - Present". References are usually "available on request" or omitted.

## Germany (de-DE)
<!-- tags: de-DE, germany, german, deutsch, lebenslauf, dach, austria, switzerland -->
Document name: "Lebenslauf". Usually tabular, one to two pages, reverse-chronological. Common headings: "Berufserfahrung", "Ausbildung", "Kenntnisse" / "Fähigkeiten", "Sprachkenntnisse", "Zertifikate". A photo was traditionally included but is optional under anti-discrimination law (AGG); do not add one on the candidate's behalf. Dates in "MM/JJJJ" format (e.g. "01/2022 - heute"). Precise, factual tone; avoid exaggerated self-praise. Language levels via CEFR (e.g. "Englisch: C1"). A dated signature at the bottom is a traditional but optional element.

## France (fr-FR)
<!-- tags: fr-FR, france, french, francais -->
Document name: "CV". One page is common for early-career candidates, two for experienced ones. Common headings: "Profil", "Expérience professionnelle", "Formation", "Compétences", "Langues", "Centres d'intérêt". A photo is optional. Language levels via CEFR. French grandes écoles and degree names are usually kept in French with a short equivalent if helpful.

## Spain (es-ES)
<!-- tags: es-ES, spain, spanish, espana -->
Document name: "currículum vitae" / "CV". One to two pages. Use Peninsular Spanish vocabulary: "ordenador", "móvil", "grado" for a bachelor's degree (e.g. "Grado en Ingeniería Informática"), "máster". Headings: "Perfil", "Experiencia profesional", "Formación", "Habilidades", "Idiomas". The Europass format is known but not required. Photos are optional.

## Netherlands (nl-NL)
<!-- tags: nl-NL, netherlands, dutch, holland -->
Concise, direct CVs of one to two pages. English-language CVs are widely accepted, especially in tech. No photo needed. A short profile at the top and clear, factual bullets are preferred over elaborate language.
